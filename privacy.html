PayPilot Finance Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

1. Introduction

PayPilot Finance helps users organize personal expenses, manage budgets, and develop financial habits. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

2. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, information may include your email address, account authentication details, expense entries, budget information, and preferences such as your language setting.

3. Expense and Budget Data

Expense and budget information is designed to be stored locally on your device. This policy must be updated if any of this information is transmitted to or stored by a server, cloud service, analytics provider, or other third party.

4. Account and Sign-In

PayPilot Finance supports email and password sign-in and may support sign-in through Apple or Google. Authentication information is processed as necessary to authenticate your account. Apple and Google may process information under their own privacy policies when you choose their sign-in services.

5. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide app features, authenticate users, save preferences, maintain app security, and respond to support requests. Information should not be used for additional purposes unless disclosed and permitted by applicable law.

6. Sharing and Third Parties

We do not intend to sell personal information. Authentication providers and other service providers may process information when necessary to provide features you choose. The final policy must identify any additional services used by the app, including analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or subscription management.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Expense and budget information stored locally can generally be removed by deleting that information within the app or removing the app from your device. Account information may be handled by the authentication provider. Users must be able to initiate account deletion from within the app. Any data retained for legal or security reasons will be handled according to applicable requirements.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

PayPilot Finance is not intended to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the app's features and data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or support, visit our support page.
